
# 📊 Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis Dashboard is an interactive Power BI data visualization project designed to analyze insurance policies, claim amounts, claim frequency, and risk patterns.

The dashboard helps insurance companies understand customer behavior, identify claim trends, evaluate risk factors, and make data-driven business decisions.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze insurance policies and claims.
- Monitor total claim amounts and claim frequency.
- Identify high-risk vehicle categories.
- Compare claims across different car manufacturers.
- Analyze claims based on education and marital status.
- Understand claim distribution across vehicle usage types.
- Support better risk management and business decisions.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. Insurance Policy Overview
Displays important insurance performance metrics:
- Total number of policies
- Claim frequency
- Total claim amount
- Average claim amount

### 2. Claim Amount by Car Use
A donut chart displays the distribution of insurance claim amounts across vehicle usage categories.

- Private Use: 150.4M
- Commercial Use: 37.41M

### 3. Claim Amount by Car Make
A horizontal bar chart compares insurance claim amounts across different automobile manufacturers.

Major brands include:
- Ford
- Chevrolet
- Dodge
- Toyota
- GMC
- Mitsubishi
- Mazda
- Pontiac

### 4. Claim Amount by Coverage Zone
A donut chart analyzes insurance claim amounts across different coverage zones, including urban, rural, suburban, and highway-related categories.

### 5. Claim Amount Trend Analysis
A line chart illustrates claim amount trends over time, helping identify patterns and fluctuations.

### 6. Claim Amount by Education
A pie chart shows the distribution of insurance claims across education levels.

- Bachelor's Degree: 94.02M
- High School: 51.8M
- Master's Degree: 28.31M
- PhD: 13.69M

### 7. Claim Amount by Marital Status
A table presents insurance claim amounts across different marital status categories.

- Single
- Married
- Divorced
- Separated

### 8. Interactive Filtering
The dashboard includes a Coverage Zone filter that allows users to explore insurance claims for selected geographical categories.

## 🔍 Key Insights

- Private-use vehicles account for the largest portion of claim amounts.
- Ford and Chevrolet show high claim amounts among the displayed car manufacturers.
- Bachelor's degree holders represent the largest claim amount among education categories.
- Single policyholders contribute the highest claim amount among marital status categories.
- Interactive visualizations help identify insurance claim patterns.
